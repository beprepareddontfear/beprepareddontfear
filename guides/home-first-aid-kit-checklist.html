What should go in a home first aid kit?

The American Red Cross lists 19 basic items for a family of four, including 25 adhesive bandages, sterile gauze pads, tape, gloves, tweezers and a thermometer, plus your own medicines and emergency phone numbers. Check it regularly and replace used or expired items.

What personal items should I add?

  • Any regular medicines your household takes
  • A written list of emergency phone numbers
  • Anything your doctor or health-care provider recommends for your family

How do I look after the kit?

  • Inspect it regularly.
  • Check expiry dates and replace anything that has expired or been used.
  • Keep it where everyone in the household can find it, and keep medicines out of children's reach.
Note. The Red Cross lists aspirin, but this guide gives no advice on using any medicine. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before giving any medicine, especially to children. A first aid kit does not replace first aid training or emergency care. In a serious emergency, call your local emergency number.
