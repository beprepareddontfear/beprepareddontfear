Everyday preparedness, without the fear.

Evenkeel shows ordinary households how to get ready for power cuts, water cuts and other disruption, using things they already have and a few smart extras. Practical steps, named sources, no scare tactics.

Start here: your 5-minute check

  • Do you have 3 days of water for everyone at home?
  • Could you eat for 3 days without a fridge or cooker?
  • Do you have a torch, spare batteries and a radio?
  • Is a power bank charged and your phone numbers written down?
  • Are your important papers copied and kept dry?
