Sources and editorial policy

Every guide names the sources it uses and shows when it was last updated.

  • We prefer government preparedness agencies, the Red Cross, the WHO and university extension services.
  • Where sources disagree, we show both and suggest the cautious figure.
  • We never give medical dosing, water-treatment claims or untested food-preservation methods.
  • We correct errors promptly and update the date.
